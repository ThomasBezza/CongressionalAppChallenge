# Demonstration walkthrough

Use a host and two willing participants on separate browser views. Use headphones
when devices are nearby. The scenes simulate conversations at real local businesses;
the app does not send orders or reservations.

1. Host chooses café or bowling, creates a room and shares the invitation.
2. Participants join with aliases. Host admits them and assigns the two scene roles.
3. Read the venue reference. The customer/caller privately chooses what to request.
4. Optionally join audio and allow the microphone. Everyone starts muted.
5. Both participants choose I’m ready; host starts.
6. Speak naturally. Request a moment on one device; show the shared message on the other.
7. Clear the request when ready. Listener asks missing details and reads back the request.
8. Optionally share and review an order ticket or reservation slip. Confirm, correct or skip.
9. Retry with fresh readiness, or end the room for everyone. Confirm closed views and
   microphone cleanup.

Café: Noble Coffee & Tea in Noblesville. Booking: Pinheads in Fishers. Practice menu
modifiers, reservation duration, days and available times are simplified simulation
choices. Venue references link to the official sources. No business affiliation is claimed.
